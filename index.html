Assistant Professor
Actuarial Science & Mathematical FinanceUniversity of Amsterdam · Amsterdam School of Economics

I am an Assistant Professor in the Quantitative Economics section at the University of Amsterdam, which I joined in September 2026.

My research lies at the intersection of machine learning, stochastic control and mathematical finance. I develop methods for high-dimensional problems in finance and insurance, with interests ranging from optimal trading and hedging to mortality risk and variable annuities.

Previously, I was a RiskLab postdoctoral researcher at ETH Zürich, working with Patrick Cheridito. I obtained my PhD in Financial and Insurance Mathematics at UCLouvain (ISBA), supervised by Donatien Hainaut.
